# Report a problem

Have a technical problem, a question, or a documentation correction? Check the [FAQ](FAQ.md), or send a report.

## Create a report

Enter your contact email, subject, category, and description. Version information and steps to reproduce help us understand the problem.

## Before sending

- Include Jardi, macOS, and iOS versions and the steps you took.
- Describe the expected and actual results, including relevant error messages.
- Remove private coordinates, device identifiers, passwords, and anything you do not want to share.
