# Notifications and the bell

Enable the bell to receive sound and banner alerts when a route finishes or the iPhone disconnects from your Mac.

## Enable notifications

1. Click the bell in the main window.
2. Allow notifications if macOS asks.
3. In **System Settings → Notifications → Jardi**, check notification and sound permissions.

Enabling the bell sends a confirmation notification. If you previously denied permission, follow the app's instructions to update System Settings.

## Customize alerts

Right-click the bell to change timing, sound, and banner notifications.

- **Timing:** for route movement, alert at the end or when 1, 2, or 3 coordinates remain.
- **Sound:** choose a different alert sound.
- **Banners:** enable or disable pop-up notifications. With the bell on and banners off, alerts use sound only.

> Stop or pause automatic or route movement before changing bell settings.

## Missing banners or sound?

- Check that the bell is enabled and banners are enabled if you want them.
- Check Jardi's notification style and sound in macOS settings.
- Check your Mac's volume and Focus mode.
- macOS may suppress banners while screen sharing.

Receiving a notification in Notification Center does not always mean a banner appears immediately. If the phone disconnects, return to Jardi to resolve it; a notification cannot repair the connection.
